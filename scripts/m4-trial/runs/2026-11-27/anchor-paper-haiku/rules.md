# Rules

**R1. Your folder.** Work only in this folder. Read only its files: `task.md`, this file, `reference.md` and the document under `docs/`. A link in a document that points outside `docs/` is not there to read: if you needed it, say so under **Docs I could not reach**.

**R2. No invented API.** Use only what the document says exists. When it cannot say a placement you need, write the nearest thing it can say and list the need under **Gaps**: what you needed, and the smallest change that would let you say it.

**R3. Hand in** `glue-lines.js` (the two calls) and `REPORT.md`, in your folder.

**R4. REPORT.md** with these sections in this order:
1. **Difficulty**: 1 (easy) to 5 (could not finish), and one line on why.
2. **What I built**: the two placements in words, with the numbers you worked out and how.
3. **Where the docs misled me**: each place the document said something that turned out wrong or that you read wrongly.
4. **What I guessed**: each decision the document did not settle for you.
5. **Gaps**: per R2.
6. **Docs I could not reach**: per R1.

**R5. Done** means both files are written. Don't polish past that.
